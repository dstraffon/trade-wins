    Data. Player and draft-pick trade legs from the open nba-trades-database project, which scraped them from Basketball-Reference. Covers trades through the February 2026 deadline.

    A ribbon counts trades in which two teams swapped at least one player or draft pick. Three-team deals add a link for every pair that exchanged something. Relocated and renamed franchises are drawn under their current name (Sonics with the Thunder, the 2002–13 New Orleans Hornets with the Pelicans, Bobcats and the original Hornets with Charlotte) and shown with their name at the time.

    Gaps. Trades that moved only cash, trade exceptions, or picks that never turned into a player can be missing or show only part of the deal. Signings, waivers and free agency aren't trades, so a dashed line in a player's path means they changed teams some other way. A few legs in the source were corrected by hand where they were clearly mis-scraped.
